## Brand & Style

This design system embodies pure atmospheric clarity, scientific serenity, and mindful utility. Designed for individuals seeking control over their personal environmental biometrics—air quality, atmospheric pressure, allergen density, and thermal comfort—it balances the precision of an aerospace instrument with the tactile tranquility of premium Scandinavian product design.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Minimalism** with **iOS-inspired Swiss clarity**. It repudiates flashy high-contrast gimmicks, neon tech glow, and AI ornamentation in favor of crisp delineation, wide breathing room, deliberate hierarchy, and weightless, flat elevation. The experience should feel like stepping into an open, mountain-air pavilion: crystalline, calming, and effortlessly legible.

## Colors

The palette revolves strictly around airy optical blues, pristine whites, and deep marine ink, keeping color temperature cohesive across data visualizations and layout containers.

### Functional Roles & Assignments
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F9FD`)**: The universal backdrop evoking ambient daylight and open sky.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary cards, panels, and actionable modular tiles.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#F0F6FD`)**: Inset containers, secondary modules, and table headers.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#EAF3FC`)**: Selected rows, toggle tracks, active navigation bars, and chip fills.
- **Hairline Border (`#DCE7F2`)**: Fine-tuned containment line for surfaces, dividers, and input borders.
- **Text Primary (`#1D3553`)**: High-contrast, deep oceanic navy for titles, metrics, and core content.
- **Text Secondary (`#6B7C91`)**: Soft slate-blue for metadata, secondary indicators, and micro-labels.
- **Brand Primary (`#3678C8`)**: Used for high-intent interactive states, focus indicators, and key graph lines.
- **Brand Secondary (`#82B4EA`)**: Subtle accent, range tracks, secondary plot points, and hover fills.

### Environmental Status Accents
Environmental status alerts remain measured and muted, avoiding aggressive neon tones:
- **Optimal / Pure (`#2C9E8E`)**: Muted blue-teal for pure air quality (AQI 0–50) and verified states.
- **Moderate / Caution (`#D9822B`)**: Restrained amber for moderate contaminants and variable readings.
- **Hazard / Warning (`#D9534F`)**: Muted coral-brick for elevated pollution spikes and active alerts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes tabular legibility, natural human curves, and crisp scanning. Plus Jakarta Sans provides precise geometric balance paired with friendly humanist terminals, echoing modern iOS system patterns.

- **Numerals**: For core environmental indexes (AQI, temperature, barometric pressure), `metric-numeral` uses a light 300 weight to prevent dense visual clumping, letting the magnitude of the number breathe against empty space.
- **Labels & Micro-headers**: Set `label-sm` and `label-md` in semi-bold with slightly expanded letter tracking (+0.02em to +0.04em), providing high legibility at diminutive scales without harsh uppercase transformations.
- **Reading Comfort**: Body copy balances tight vertical leading with ample letter form clearance, using `#6B7C91` for subtext to maintain high visual contrast without optic fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an adaptive multi-column fluid container with anchored side padding. It favors high internal breathing room within cards to highlight atmospheric data.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1rem` (16px), `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Single-column card stacking. Bottom tab navigation anchors tool switching.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, `margin-tablet: 2rem` (32px), `gutter-tablet: 1.25rem` (20px). Two-column modular grid for sensor feeds.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12 columns, max-width 1280px, `margin-desktop: 3rem` (48px), `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem` (24px). Sidebar utility with asymmetric dashboard cards.

### Spacing Rules
- Never cluster telemetry metrics directly adjacent to borders; cards enforce an internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile.
- Section dividers use empty vertical distance (`space-xl`) rather than solid full-bleed lines wherever possible.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system excludes heavy drop shadows, harsh skeuomorphic bevels, and saturated ambient halos. Depth is articulated purely through surface tonal contrast and refined hairpins.

- **Stacking Surfaces**: Visual hierarchy ascends through tonal lightness:
  - Base: `#F5F9FD` (Backdrop)
  - Level 1: `#FFFFFF` (Main Cards & Modules)
  - Level 2: `#F0F6FD` or `#EAF3FC` (Sub-wells within cards and active state containers)
- **Low-Contrast Outlines**: Every card, popover, and floating item is enclosed with a 1px solid border in `#DCE7F2`.
- **Soft Environmental Depth**: Floating dialogs, menus, and sticky navigation use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with oceanic ink: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(29, 53, 83, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(29, 53, 83, 0.02)`.
- **Frost Layering**: Modals and floating navigation bars leverage subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`) over an 85% alpha `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F9FD` fill.

## Shapes

The interface balances iOS-style fluid squircles with architectural restraint. 

- **Cards & Data Modules**: Base radius of `1rem` (16px), delivering a soft, non-aggressive touchpoint for daily tracking widgets.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Selectors, Action Wells)**: Set to `0.5rem` (8px) for crisp interaction affordance.
- **Status Pills, Metric Tags & Floating Badges**: Full geometric pill (`9999px`) to immediately differentiate contextual indicators from layout containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid fill `#3678C8`, label `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`, 0.5rem border radius. Hover: `#2E68B0`. Active scale: 0.98. No shadow; clarity driven by contrast.
- **Secondary / Soft**: Fill `#EAF3FC`, label `#3678C8`, border 1px solid transparent. Hover: `#DCE7F2`.
- **Ghost**: Fill transparent, label `#1D3553`, hover fill `#F0F6FD`.
- **Icon Buttons**: Uniform 40×40px circle or square (`0.5rem` radius), border 1px solid `#DCE7F2`, icon stroke 1.5px in `#1D3553`.

### Cards & Telemetry Tiles
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DCE7F2`, border-radius `1rem`. Internal padding `1.5rem`.
- Data tiles feature an upper-left title in `label-md` (`#6B7C91`), a top-right line icon, the primary reading in `metric-numeral` or `headline-xl` (`#1D3553`), and an inline status pill at the bottom edge.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Enclosed with a full pill radius (`9999px`).
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DCE7F2`, text `#6B7C91`.
- Active: Background `#EAF3FC`, border 1px solid `#82B4EA`, text `#3678C8`, weight `600`.

### Data Visualizations & Sparklines
- Chart baselines and gridlines use `#DCE7F2` at 0.75px stroke with low dash frequency.
- Line charts utilize single-pixel to 2-pixel vector paths in `#3678C8` or `#2C9E8E`.
- Gradient area fills beneath curves are strictly limited to vertical fades from 12% opacity of the line color to 0% at the baseline.

### Input Fields
- Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#DCE7F2`, radius `0.5rem`, text `#1D3553` (`body-md`), placeholder `#6B7C91`.
- Focus state: border 1.5px solid `#3678C8`, accompanied by a 2px outer ambient ring in `rgba(54, 120, 200, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: 20×20px, 4px corner radius. Unchecked has `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#DCE7F2` border. Checked has `#3678C8` fill with an optical white check icon.
- Radios: 20×20px circular ring. Selected has an inner centered `#3678C8` solid dot (8px diameter) spaced cleanly inside the white housing.

### Environmental Status Badges
- Pill shape (`9999px`), padding 4px 10px, typography `label-sm`.
- **Pure Air**: Background `rgba(44, 158, 142, 0.12)`, text `#2C9E8E`.
- **Moderate**: Background `rgba(217, 130, 43, 0.12)`, text `#D9822B`.
- **Alert**: Background `rgba(217, 83, 79, 0.12)`, text `#D9534F`.
